How I work

Four stages, each with something to look at — you don't wait a month to see the first result.

01

Understanding the task

I look at how the business gets clients today and what actually gets in the way. The output is a site structure and a list of what is genuinely needed.

02

Design and prototype

I produce a visual specification and mock up the key screens before writing code. Changes at this point are free and fast.

03

Development

Frontend, backend, admin panel, integrations with CRM, messengers and AI models. Work in progress is always visible on a staging domain.

04

Launch and support

Domain, hosting, analytics, indexing. After that, changes as the business grows — without rewriting the project from scratch.
